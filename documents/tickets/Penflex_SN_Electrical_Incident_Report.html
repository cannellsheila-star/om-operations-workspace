Incident record

The reported works involved the PV plant, BESS, VT1 and Eskom incomer. During an attempted application of earth on the Eskom incomer, an electrical fault was suspected and the upstream Eskom protection operated.

SN Electrical recorded that it was not authorised to operate medium-voltage equipment. The site was placed into a controlled safe state while evidence and the sequence of events were investigated.

Current operating position

With the Eskom path unavailable, Penflex is operating islanded: PV and BESS support daytime operation and a 650 kVA generator provides backup and night supply.

Rectification work under way

  • Replace the 24 kV Schneider RM AirSet / affected switching equipment.
  • Repair the BESS feeder cable and replace the cable between the VT and RM AirSet.
  • Reterminate the VT connection.
  • Complete phase-rotation, insulation-resistance, VLF, protection and functional testing before commissioning and restoration.

Required ticket updates

Add the investigation evidence, rectification test results, commissioning evidence and final restoration status to TKT-2026-0004 as they are completed.
